# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
REDTEXT Generator community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- **Using this project, its issues, or its discussions to plan, solicit, or
  facilitate unauthorized or illegal social-engineering activity.** REDTEXT is a
  defensive and educational tool; the community will not assist with attacks on
  non-consenting targets.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers via GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the
privacy and security of the reporter.

## Attribution
